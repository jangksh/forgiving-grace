1. Made to order

Every Forgiving Grace garment is printed for you individually after your order is placed. Nothing sits in a warehouse. This keeps waste low, and it is also why the timelines and return rules below differ slightly from a mass-stocked retailer.

2. Processing and delivery times

StageTypical time
Production (printing and quality check)2–5 business days
Delivery after dispatch (United States)5–10 business days (US)
Delivery after dispatch (international)10–20 business days, plus customs clearance

Production time is separate from delivery time, so please allow for both. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays begin production on the next business day. Times may be longer during launches, sales and peak holiday periods.

3. Shipping rates and destinations

Shipping is calculated at checkout and shown before you pay. Sales tax is applied where required by law.

For international orders, any customs duties, import taxes or handling fees charged by the destination country are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in the price you pay us.

4. Tracking your order

You will receive an order reference (format FG-YYMMDD-XXXX) as soon as your order is submitted, and a tracking link by email once your parcel is dispatched. Keep the reference number — it is the fastest way for us to find your order.

5. Returns and exchanges

You may request a return or size exchange within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unworn, unwashed, undamaged, free of odours and marks, and in its original packaging with any tags attached.

Because each garment is produced individually, we cannot accept returns simply because you changed your mind about a design after wearing it. The following are not eligible for return or refund:

  • Items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.
  • Personalised or custom-made items (names, custom verses, bespoke artwork).
  • Items returned more than 30 days after delivery.
  • Items returned without contacting us first (see section 7).

For approved returns that are not our error, the cost of return shipping is paid by the customer, and the original shipping charge is not refunded. Exchanges are subject to availability of the requested size or colour.

6. Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your item arrives with a printing defect, a manufacturing fault, transit damage, or is simply not what you ordered, we fix it at our cost — a free reprint and reshipment, or a full refund if you prefer.

Please contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order reference and clear photographs of the item and the shipping label. You will not be asked to pay return shipping in these cases.

7. How to start a return

  1. Email — with the subject line "Return" or "Exchange".
  2. Include your order reference, the item and size, and the reason for the return. Attach photographs if the item is damaged or misprinted.
  3. We reply within 2 business days with a return authorisation and the return address.
  4. Ship the item back using a trackable service. Items sent back without a return authorisation cannot be processed.

8. Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected — usually within 3 business days of arrival — we email you the outcome. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Your bank or card issuer typically takes a further 5–10 business days to post the credit to your account.

If a refund has not appeared after 10 business days, contact your bank first, then email us and we will follow it up.

9. Wrong or incomplete addresses

Please check your shipping address carefully before paying. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incorrect or incomplete, we will contact you and reship it once you have covered the reshipping cost. Address changes can only be made before production begins, so email us immediately if you spot a mistake.

10. Lost or stolen packages

If tracking shows a parcel as delivered but you have not received it, please check with your household and neighbours and with your local carrier first. If it still cannot be found, contact us within 14 days of the delivery date and we will open an investigation with the carrier. Parcels confirmed lost in transit are reshipped at our cost.
