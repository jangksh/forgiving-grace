Forgiving Grace ("Forgiving Grace", "we", "us") operates this website and sells faith-based apparel. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and what choices you have. By using this site or placing an order, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Order details — name, email address, phone number (optional), shipping address, and the product, size, colour and quantity you selected.
  • Messages — anything you write in our contact form, including your name, email address, chosen topic and message body.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard log data such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the pages you view on this site.
  • Cookie and pixel data, only where you have accepted analytics cookies (see section 3).

We never collect or store payment card details. Card numbers, expiry dates and security codes are entered directly on the payment processor's own secure checkout page and are never transmitted to or stored by this website.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, produce, ship and support your order.
  • To send order confirmations, payment links, shipping notifications and service updates.
  • To answer questions you send through the contact form.
  • To handle returns, exchanges, reprints and refunds.
  • To understand how the store is used so we can improve it (aggregate analytics).
  • To meet tax, accounting and other legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own independent marketing.

3. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies and local storage are used to remember your cookie choice and to keep the site working. These are always on because the site cannot function without them.

Analytics and advertising cookies (such as Google Analytics 4 and the Meta pixel) are loaded only after you press "Accept" on our cookie banner. If you press "Decline", no analytics or advertising scripts are loaded at all. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which brings the banner back.

These tools tell us things like which products are viewed most and how many visitors reach the checkout step. They may set their own cookies and process data under their own privacy policies: Google and Meta.

4. Who we share information with

We share the minimum information needed with service providers who help us run the store:

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect our rights, safety or property.

5. How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed to fulfil the order and to satisfy tax and accounting requirements (generally up to seven years in the United States). Contact-form messages are kept for up to two years. You may ask us to delete information earlier where we are not legally required to keep it.

6. Your privacy rights

California residents (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request a copy, to request deletion, to correct inaccurate information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

Residents of the EEA/UK (GDPR). You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of your information, and the right to data portability. Our legal bases are performance of a contract (orders), consent (analytics and marketing cookies) and legitimate interests (running and securing the store).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at —. We will respond within 45 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

7. Security

This site is served over HTTPS and order data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Access to order records is limited to people who need it to fulfil orders. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

This store is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our store grows or as the law changes. The "last updated" date at the top of this page always shows the current version. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.
